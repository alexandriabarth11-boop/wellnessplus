Welcome to Wellness Plus Pharmacy & Clinic

Wellness Plus Pharmacy & Clinic is a privately owned community pharmacy and walk-in clinic located at 14 Main Street, Spanish Town, St. Catherine. At Wellness Plus Pharmacy & Clinic, your comfort and individual care always come first. We bring compassionate, professional medical care directly to your community, allowing you to get treated and pick up your prescriptions on a single convenient visit. Our team of board-certified doctors and experienced nurses takes the time to listen to your needs and provide personalised treatment. We accept major health insurance plans, including NHF, making quality care simple, accessible, and affordable for all. Our team is always here to support you every step of the way with whatever you need.

Our Medical & Pharmacy Services

We offer a comprehensive range of outpatient clinical and pharmaceutical services tailored to meet your everyday health needs:

  • Primary & Urgent Care: Our clinic offers comprehensive general practitioner consultations to address your everyday health concerns. We provide prompt acute illness treatment so you can recover quickly and get back on your feet. Patients living with long-term conditions benefit from dedicated chronic disease management for hypertension and diabetes. We also perform routine health screenings to help detect potential issues early and maintain your overall well-being.
  • Comprehensive Pharmacy Solutions: Our pharmacy team provides fast and reliable prescription filling and refills for all your medication needs. You can schedule a thorough medication therapy review to ensure your prescriptions are working safely and effectively together. Our knowledgeable pharmacists are always available to offer trusted over-the-counter advice for minor ailments. We also offer structured medication adherence programs to help you stay consistent with your daily treatment plan.
  • Preventive Health & Vaccines: Protect your long-term health with our full range of routine adult immunisations. We provide gentle, timely pediatric vaccinations to keep children safe as they grow. Stay prepared during peak seasons with convenient flu and pneumonia shots. If you are planning an international trip, our team offers specialised travel health consultations to ensure you have the necessary protection before you go.
  • Specialty Diagnostics & Wellness: We prioritise your emotional and psychological well-being through compassionate mental health support. Our specialists offer personalised nutritional counselling to help you build sustainable, healthy eating habits. You can conveniently access basic lab tests, including blood glucose and cholesterol checks, right at our facility. We also provide comprehensive dental visits and oral health care to keep your smile healthy.

Health & Wellness Tips

1. Stay Hydrated Daily

Drink at least 2 litres of water daily to maintain energy and support body functions.

2. Eat Balanced Meals

Incorporate whole grains, lean proteins, fruits, and fresh vegetables into your daily diet.

3. Keep Moving

Aim for at least 30 minutes of moderate exercise.

4. Prioritise Rest

Get 7 to 8 hours of quality sleep each night to help your immune system repair and recharge.
