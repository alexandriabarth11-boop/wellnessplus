Inside Our Facility & Community Programs

Take a closer look at our modern clinical consultation rooms, fully stocked dispensary, and recent community health fairs.

State-of-the-Art Pharmacy Dispensary

Our dispensary is equipped with automated inventory tracking and temperature-controlled storage to guarantee the potency and safety of every medication. Our pharmacists are always available at the private counselling window to answer questions about dosage and drug interactions.

Outpatient Examination & Diagnostic Suites

Designed for patient comfort and privacy, our examination rooms feature modern diagnostic equipment for ECGs, Nebulizer treatments, wound care, and maternal health checks. Minimal wait times ensure you get back to your day quickly.

Annual Community Wellness Outreach

Beyond our clinic walls, Wellness Plus hosts quarterly free health fairs offering complimentary blood pressure checks, nutrition counselling, and back-to-school medicals for residents.
